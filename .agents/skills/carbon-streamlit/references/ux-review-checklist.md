# Implementation UX checklist

- Is the page question still obvious within seconds?
- Are primary outcomes more prominent than support metadata?
- Do modules share content boundaries, gutters, and alignment?
- Is each section answering one question?
- Are primary, secondary, reset, download, and details actions visually distinct?
- Are empty, warning, loading, success, and error states understandable?
- Are charts readable and semantically colored?
- Does the change preserve filters, navigation, table pagination/downloads, and data behavior?
- Does the result remain restrained at desktop, laptop, and tablet widths?
